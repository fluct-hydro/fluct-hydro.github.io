About the programme

Systems made of many interacting particles fluctuate randomly around their macroscopic behaviour. Fluctuating hydrodynamics looks for stochastic continuum equations that capture these mesoscopic fluctuations. Such equations come up in fluids, interacting diffusions, active matter and many other settings.

The resulting equations are often singular: the noise is rough, the nonlinearities are strong, and even the notion of a solution can be delicate. Recent progress has started to make nonlinear models, such as the Dean–Kawasaki equation and Landau–Lifshitz-type fluctuating hydrodynamics, open to rigorous analysis.

The programme aims to consolidate this emerging field. It brings together researchers in probability, analysis and related areas.
